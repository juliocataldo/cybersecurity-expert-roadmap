Policy Creation Workflow

1.Visualize application flows
2.Create an alerting segmentation policy
3.Monitor and revise your policy (15 a 20 dias, mas pode demorar mais dependendo da aplicação)
4.Transition Alert Rules to Block Rules

Ring fencing - Trust intern communication (allow any) and analyze incoming traffic and outgoing traffic.
Tier segmentation - Not Trust intern communication too - In this template Tier Segment. will just create existent communication with allow and block others. Third level can be any label you choose (i.e Role, Location, Departmente, etc)

Templates

  1. DIY --> Templates that generate Policies for securing specified groups of assets according to a policy type., for example templates include RING FENCING and app, or whitelisting out bounds flows for an app. DIY template may also include built-in policies in the template (such as baseline alert). In DIY templates we create an allow rules directly from traffic, as visualized in Reveal.
  2. Template --> Templates that Generate Policies for know Applications - In Template we use built in best practice policies for specific applications

Ransomware Templates

Ransomware is a malware that infects a machine, encrypts all of its data, and won't allow decryption unless the victim pays a free. Consequence: Down-time, theft os IP/data, reputational damage, regulatory fines

Ransomware templates provide users with a highly effective protective layer against ransomware. With just few clicks, and no risk of downtime, users may quickly create policies that reduce the network based attack surface of common ransomware techniques.

10 of the 11 best-know Ransomware attack are windows only, and most of the market talks about the impacto of ransomware on windows

  • Ransomware attacks seek high-privileged credentials (usually domain admin) in order to spread.
  • The attacker finds a high-privileged credential by means of Password leaks, mimikatz & Isass, cached logons, etc
  • Most ransomware spread techniques use Windows Service Interfaces.
  • A singles windows process may run multiple Windows services (svchos.exe)
  • Some services use dynamic (ephemeral) ports (49152-65535)
  • This was not practical as it would break the system.

Ransomware Templates

  • Templates to prevent lateral movement with high-privileged users
  • Templates that block communication from 'low-privileged' machines running task-scheduler and remote power shell to high privileged machines.
  • Templates to mitigate risks of one-day vulnerabilities
  • Templates to block common ransomware techniques
  • Most ransom uses a C&C server to get commands and report logs. Some ransom uses a native windows tool called BITS (Background Intelligent Transfer File)
  • One of the most common ransomware techniques is to clean the windows event log. - Guardicore includes a template that - Guardicore can prevents this.
  • Templates Task Scheduler
  • Templates Print Spooler
  • Template Event Logs
  • Template WinRM - Windows Remote Management

Bulk Operations

Perform bulk operations on multiple rules by first filtering the rules to obtain those on which you want to perform the bulk operation, then selecting the Bulk operations button at the top of screen and choosing an option.

Policy Suggestions

Policy Suggestions allows to tune your existing policy by analyzing flows that were alerted or blocked by the current policy, and suggesting corresponding Allow rules which you may review and accept/dismiss.

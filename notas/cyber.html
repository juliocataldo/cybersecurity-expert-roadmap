Cyber

#offsec #tools

Type: Hacking

Cybersecurity professionals utilize a variety of tools to perform their duties effectively. Here are some main categories of tools commonly used in the field of cybersecurity:

  1. Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT) Tools:
  • Nessus
  • OpenVAS
  • QualysGuard
  • Burp Suite
  • Metasploit
  1. Network Security Tools:
  • Wireshark
  • Nmap
  • Snort
  • Suricata
  • Bro/Zeek
  1. Intrusion Detection and Prevention Systems (IDS/IPS):
  • Snort
  • Suricata
  • Cisco Firepower
  • McAfee Intrushield
  1. Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) Tools:
  • Splunk
  • LogRhythm
  • ArcSight
  • ELK Stack (Elasticsearch, Logstash, Kibana)
  1. Endpoint Protection Tools:
  • Symantec Endpoint Protection
  • McAfee Endpoint Security
  • CrowdStrike Falcon
  • Microsoft Defender ATP
  1. Web Application Security Tools:
  • OWASP ZAP
  • Acunetix
  • AppScan
  • Burp Suite
  1. Encryption and Cryptography Tools:
  • OpenSSL
  • GnuPG (GNU Privacy Guard)
  • Hashcat
  • VeraCrypt
  1. Forensic Tools:
  • EnCase
  • Autopsy
  • Volatility
  • FTK (Forensic Toolkit)
  1. Threat Intelligence Tools:
  • VirusTotal
  • Shodan
  • Recorded Future
  • ThreatConnect
  1. Security Assessment and Compliance Tools:
  • Qualys
  • Rapid7 Nexpose
  • Tenable.io
  • OpenSCAP

These are just a few examples of the tools utilized in the cybersecurity domain. The choice of tools depends on the specific tasks, goals, and preferences of the cybersecurity professional or organization. It's important to continuously explore and stay up to date with new tools and technologies emerging in the cybersecurity field to adapt to evolving threats and challenges.
